Logan Till: 4/25/26

Tron

Tron was one of the first movies to use computer generated enviorment and assets in a movie. The story is entertaining and engaging keeps people entertained. It is a cult classic that should be rememberd.

7/10

Tron: Legacy

Tron: Legacy was one of the first movie to have digital version of an actor on screen with the same actor. With music by daft punk and amazing vizuals this is one of my facvoirte movies. Although, the writing could have been better I believe it is a masterpeice.

9.5/10

Tron: Ares

Tron: Ares was great looking and it also had great music by nine inch nails. I give it less score because I did not like the acting from Jared Leto. Was a good continuation of the series.

6.5/10

Trivia

    1. Tron was inspired by png
    2. The crowd voices during the disk battle in the beginning of the movie are actually the voices of the audience during the panel for the film at the 2010 San Diego Comic-Con. The audience was instructed to stomp and chant to prompts shown on the large video screen, and the sound was recorded by techs from Skywalker Sound
    3. Oranges and the orange tree are used for testing the ability to synthesize objects from the digital world. In Tron (1982) the very first item digitized was an orange.
